Where the sounds in this mod came from
======================================

Every sound shipped with Elden Search is CC0 (public domain). Nothing here
requires attribution, but it is given anyway, because the people who made
these put them out for free and that is worth writing down.

  Kenney - "Interface Sounds" (CC0)
  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Full licence text: LICENSE-Kenney-CC0.txt

  OpenGameArt - "100 CC0 SFX" (CC0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx

  artisticdude - "Swishes Sound Pack" (CC0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack
  (the delete tick and the clear sweep, esb_sound_delete/clear.wav)

The files were shortened, resampled and pitched to sit under the game's own
menu clicks. The originals are unmodified at the links above.

The typeface is EB Garamond by Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo, under the
SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 - see EBGaramond-OFL.txt.
