WHAT THIS FOLDER IS
===================

These files are not part of the mod's content - nothing in here does
anything in the game, and you do not need to open or read any of it.

Elden Search is built with free, open-source components (the Dear ImGui and
MinHook libraries compiled into the DLL, the EB Garamond typeface, and a set
of CC0 sounds). Some of their licences require that their text travels with
anything that ships them; the rest are included to give credit and to
document where everything came from.

Please leave the folder as it is - it is the mod's proof that everything in
it is properly licensed.
